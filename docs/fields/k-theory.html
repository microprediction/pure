MSC 19 · Algebra

K-theory

Vector bundles (or their algebraic analogues) classified via stable-isomorphism groups.

Founded: 1957 — Grothendieck's algebraic K-theory (K₀); topological K-theory follows with Atiyah–Hirzebruch, 1959–61 — right at the 1960 boundary

The claim

No credible dismissal found.

What happened

Topological insulators (2009) — Kitaev's K-theory classification of topological phases of matter — experimentally real (HgTe wells, Bi₂Se₃), but working physicists mostly use Chern numbers/Z₂ invariants directly rather than invoking K-theory by name.

52 years — founding (1957) to the thin, physics-only application (2009)

Verdict: No major application documented.

The other commonly cited application, D-branes in string theory, doesn't clear the real-world bar — string theory is experimentally unconfirmed. Flagged as one of the survey's genuinely tenuous cases.

Confidence: medium.

Part of the 34-field survey — microprediction/pure.
