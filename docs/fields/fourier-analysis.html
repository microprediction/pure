Fourier analysis

Representing functions as sums of sines and cosines.

Founded: 1822 — Fourier's Théorie analytique de la chaleur

The claim

No credible dismissal found. The 1807 Paris Academy committee (Lagrange, Laplace, Monge, Lacroix) objected to representing arbitrary functions as trigonometric series on rigor grounds, not utility — the underlying problem (heat conduction) was never in question.

What happened

Tide-predicting machine (1872) — Kelvin's harmonic analysis of tidal constituents — an early, concrete engineering application.

50 years — founding (1822) to Kelvin's tide predictor (1872)

Verdict: Never dismissed, applied anyway.

Confidence: medium-high.
