MSC 04 · Logic & foundations

Set theory

Cantor's theory of infinite sets: cardinals, ordinals, the continuum hypothesis, forcing.

Founded: 1874 — Cantor's first paper on the uncountability of the reals

The claim

No credible dismissal found. The famous “set theory is a disease” line usually pinned on Poincaré is documented as apocryphal (Jeremy Gray, The Mathematical Intelligencer, 1991). Kronecker's attacks on Cantor (“corrupter of youth”) target set theory's legitimacy — whether actual infinities exist at all — not its utility.

What happened

No major real-world application is well-documented today. None well-documented outside pure mathematics for the field's distinctive content (cardinal/ordinal arithmetic, forcing, large cardinals). Turing's 1936 reuse of Cantor's diagonalization is a reuse of a proof technique, not an application of transfinite set theory itself.

Verdict: No major application documented.

Confidence: medium.
