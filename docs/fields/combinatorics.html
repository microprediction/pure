MSC 05 · Logic & foundations

Combinatorics

Counting, arrangement and structure of discrete sets: enumeration, graph theory, design theory.

Founded: 1900 — No single founding event — ancient roots (Pascal's triangle, Euler's Königsberg bridges); became a systematically organized field with Fisher's design theory in the 1920s (approximate)

The claim

No credible dismissal found. Retrospectives repeat only a vague, unattributed folk memory that combinatorics was “unserious” mid-century, with no named person or exact quote. One narrow, dated anecdote: Mittag-Leffler shelved 18th-century “combinatorial school” texts under a library label “Dekadenter” (decadent) — targets one obsolete pre-1900 formalist tradition, not the modern field.

What happened

Design of experiments (1925) — R. A. Fisher's combinatorial design theory underlies randomized agricultural and clinical-trial methodology.
Reed–Solomon codes (1960) — Enumerative/finite combinatorics underlying error-correcting codes; Voyager, CDs, QR codes.

no meaningful gap — organized and applied at roughly the same time

Verdict: Never dismissed, applied anyway.

Confidence: medium.
