MSC 57/58 · Topology

Manifolds & low-dimensional topology (knot theory)

Kelvin's wrong theory that atoms were knotted vortices spawned a genuine mathematical field.

Founded: 1867 — Kelvin's “On Vortex Atoms” and Tait's resulting knot tabulation

The claim

Lord Kelvin, 1867: Proposed atoms were knotted vortices in the luminiferous aether — completely wrong physics, abandoned within decades.

Source: Kelvin, “On Vortex Atoms,” Proc. Royal Society of Edinburgh, 1867.

An unusual case: the dismissal isn't of the math but of a physical theory that happened to leave knot theory behind as its accidental, then-abandoned byproduct.

What happened

DNA topology (1990) — Topoisomerases manage DNA knotting and supercoiling (Sumners et al.).
Topological quantum computing (1997) — Kitaev's proposal to use anyons — particles that braid like knots.

123 years — founding (1867) to DNA topology (1990)

Verdict: Named dismissal, later applied.

Confidence: high.
