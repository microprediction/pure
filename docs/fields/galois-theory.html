MSC 12 · Algebra

Field theory & Galois theory

Which polynomial equations are solvable by radicals — and the finite-field arithmetic this required.

Founded: 1830 — Galois's memoir on solvability by radicals

The claim

Siméon Poisson (Paris Academy), 1831: “Neither sufficiently clear nor sufficiently developed to allow us to judge its rigour.”

Source: Poisson's 1831 Academy report, as reproduced by MacTutor History of Mathematics.

A rejection for incomprehensibility, not a claim of uselessness — a different flavor of dismissal than Hardy's. Not published until 1846, 14 years after Galois's death.

What happened

Reed–Solomon codes (1960) — Finite-field (“Galois field”) arithmetic. Voyager's telemetry, CDs, QR codes.

130 years — founding (1830) to Reed–Solomon (1960)

Verdict: Named dismissal, later applied.

Confidence: medium.
