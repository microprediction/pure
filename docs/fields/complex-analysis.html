Complex analysis

Holomorphic functions of a complex variable: Cauchy's theorem, residues, conformal mapping.

Founded: 1825 — Cauchy's integral theorem

The claim

No credible dismissal found. Cardano's “as subtle as it is useless” (1545) and Descartes coining “imaginary” (1637) as an insult both target complex numbers, roughly two centuries before complex analysis existed as a field — conflating the two would misattribute the dismissal.

What happened

AC circuit analysis (1893) — Charles Steinmetz's complex-impedance/phasor method for electrical engineering.

68 years — founding (1825) to Steinmetz (1893)

Verdict: Never dismissed, applied anyway.

Confidence: medium.
