MSC 18 · Algebra

Category theory & homological algebra

Objects, morphisms, and composition — the structural patterns common to all of mathematics.

Founded: 1945 — Eilenberg & Mac Lane's General Theory of Natural Equivalences

The claim

attributed to Norman Steenrod, 1945: “Abstract nonsense” — an affectionate in-joke, not a hostile dismissal.

Source: Attribution via Rotman (Bull. AMS 33:4, 1996) and McLarty (Br. J. Philos. Sci. 41, 1990); no primary quote from Steenrod himself has surfaced.

Coined by, and popular among, its own early practitioners — the opposite of an outside attack.

What happened

Monads in functional programming (1989) — Moggi applies category-theoretic monads to programming-language semantics; standardized as Haskell's I/O mechanism by 1996.

44 years — founding (1945) to Moggi's monads (1989)

Verdict: Named dismissal, later applied.

Confidence: medium.
