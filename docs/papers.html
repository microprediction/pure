Write-ups that take the survey's findings as input and ask what they imply for how much mathematics society should buy.

Working papers

  • Cotton, P. (2026). Mathematics and Welfare: Optimal Investment Pre and Post LLMs. Working paper — PDF · tex. The value of an unmotivated mathematical result is a sum over the uses it reaches, net of adoption cost, discounted by the lag before the match is made. Language models move all three: a result in the training corpus is available at every use, the person with the problem no longer has to be the person who knows the theorem, and the lag collapses from the 68-year median in the historical record to the day of posting. Optimal research spending has elasticity at least one in that value, and the professions that train rival human capital move the other way, so the ratio of investment in mathematics to investment in law and commerce should rise by at least an order of magnitude. Opens with a pure mathematics class of six and closes with a reply to the Fields medalists' declaration on AI and mathematics.
