Working papers

  • Cotton, P. (2026). Misaligned or Undersupplied? Pure Mathematics in the Age of LLMs. Working paper, SSRN 7452281 · PDF · tex. Twenty-five Fields medalists have declared artificial intelligence and mathematics misaligned. The wait between a mathematical result and its use was in large part transmission: a result reached a use only when someone along the way knew both the problem and the theorem, and a language model can now be that person at every use. In a minimal model of reach, adoption cost, and lag, the value of a marginal result rises by a multiple and the wedge between social and private returns widens. Opens with a pure mathematics class of six and closes with a reply to the declaration.
